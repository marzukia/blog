# Research notes: The Debasement of the AI Token

Verified by subagent research, 2026-09-30. Every fact below has a source; URLs included. Items the post currently asserts that could NOT be fully corroborated are listed at the end under CAUTIONS.

## B1 — AI-cited tech layoffs, 2025-2026

1. 2025: ~54,836 US job cuts cited AI (Challenger, Gray & Christmas), out of ~1.17-1.2M total US cuts, the highest level since 2020.
   Source: CNBC, 21 Dec 2025. https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/21/ai-job-cuts-amazon-microsoft-and-more-cite-ai-for-2025-layoffs.html
   Use: anchor stat for "the last 12 months of restructuring".

2. Jan-Aug 2026: at least 94,046 US tech layoffs, up 16.8% vs 80,486 in the same 2025 window. AI was cited in 33% of 2026 tech layoff events, up from 1% in 2024. Layoffs.fyi attributes 92,913 global 2026 layoffs (72% of the total) to AI. May 2026 was the worst month (31,513 cuts, incl. Meta's 8,000).
   Source: Crunchbase News (Layoffs.fyi, Roger Lee). https://news.crunchbase.com/layoffs/2026-layoff-numbers-rise-ai-shift-orcl-meta-amzn
   Use: the single best 2026 aggregate; the 1% to 33% jump is the headline.

3. H1 2026: AI cited in 101,743 US job-cut announcements (~23% of all cuts); tech the top sector, cuts up 83% YoY. AI's share of monthly cuts rose from 7% (Jan) to 40% (May). AI was the No.1 cited reason for four straight months.
   Source: HR Dive. https://www.hrdive.com/news/tech-layoffs-surge-83percent-h1-2026-challenger-ai-disruption/824320
   Use: acceleration trend inside 2026.

4. Named cases (all AI-cited):
   - Amazon: 14,000 corporate cuts 28 Oct 2025 (Bloomberg), +16,000 late Jan 2026 (Fortune) = ~30,000 in four months.
     https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-28/amzn-amazon-to-cut-14-000-jobs-across-corporate-workforce
   - Meta: 8,000 from 20 May 2026 (~10%), 7,000 reassigned to AI roles, 6,000 planned hires cancelled; 2026 capex guided $115-135B, nearly double 2025's $72.2B.
     https://qz.com/meta-zuckerberg-layoffs-ai-spending-cuts-050126 and https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-expects-annual-capital-expenditures-rise-superintelligence-push-2026-01-28
   - Oracle: wave 31 Mar 2026; TD Cowen est. 20,000-30,000 (~18% of 162,000) to free $8-10B for AI data centres. FY2026 10-K: headcount down ~21,000 (13%), AI "has already resulted in workforce reductions".
     https://thenextweb.com/news/oracle-layoffs-march-2026 and https://time.com/article/2026/04/30/oracle-layoffs-ai-tech-jobs
   - Block: 4,000+ cuts 26 Feb 2026 (from 10k+ to just under 6k, nearly half the staff) while Q4 gross profit up 24% YoY; shares +25% after hours.
     https://www.reuters.com/business/blocks-fourth-quarter-profit-rises-announces-over-4000-job-cuts-2026-02-26
   - Cloudflare: 1,100+ (~20%) 7 May 2026, "agentic AI-first operating model", same day as record Q1 revenue $639.8M (+34%); stock -20%. Internal AI usage up 600% in three months.
     https://qz.com/cloudflare-layoffs-ai-workforce-earnings-050826
   - Salesforce: ~4,000 support roles in 2025 (support workforce 9,000 to ~5,000), attributed to Agentforce; "no longer need[s] to actively backfill".
     https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/02/salesforce-ceo-confirms-4000-layoffs-because-i-need-less-heads-with-ai.html
   - Microsoft: ~6,000 (May 2025) + 9,000 (Jul 2025); buyout offer to ~8,750 (Apr 2026); 4,800 more (Jul 2026).
     https://fortune.com/2025/07/02/microsoft-layoffs-9000-ai
   - GitLab: ~14% (~350 of 2,580), exited 22 of 60 countries, board-approved 1 Jun 2026, while Q1 FY27 revenue +23% and gross margin 88%.
     https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/gitlab-cuts-14-of-staff-as-it-scales-its-platform-to-serve-ai-workloads
   - UPS (non-tech corroboration): 48,000 in 2025; up to 30,000 more announced 27 Jan 2026, tied to automation.
     https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/27/ups-job-cuts-amazon-unwind-turnaround-plan.html

5. Academic counterweight: Stanford Digital Economy Lab "Canaries in the Coal Mine" (rev. Aug 2026): no economy-wide displacement yet, but employment of 22-25-year-olds in highly AI-exposed occupations is 19% below trend (June 2026), widened from 15% (Jul 2025), driven by reduced hiring.
   https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26
   Use: citing it makes the section harder to dismiss as hype.

## B3 — Price increases / subsidy removals, 2024-2026

1. OpenAI GPT-4.5, Mar 2025: $75/M input, $150/M output = 30x GPT-4o input ($2.50) and 15x output ($10). OpenAI admitted it was "very expensive to run" and evaluated whether to keep serving it via API. Retired from the consumer app 27 Jun 2026.
   https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/05/openais-gpt-4-5-ai-model-comes-to-more-chatgpt-users
   Use: the frontier is not uniformly deflationary; the top of the line is priced for cost recovery.

2. GPT-4o retired from ChatGPT 13 Feb 2026 on ~2 weeks notice, citing ~0.1% daily usage, contradicting Altman's Aug-Nov 2025 "no plans to sunset 4o". The chatgpt-4o-latest API endpoint shut 16-17 Feb 2026.
   https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-is-ending-api-access-to-fan-favorite-gpt-4o-model-in-february-2026
   Use: same named service, quietly swapped, with dates.

3. OpenAI official deprecation schedule: 23 Oct 2026 (gpt-4-0613, gpt-4o-2024-05-13, o1, o1-pro, gpt-4-turbo, gpt-4.1-nano, gpt-3.5-turbo-0125, gpt-image-1); 11 Dec 2026 (early GPT-5 snapshots, o3); 26 Feb 2027 (whisper-1, gpt-4o-transcribe). Notice tiers: 6 months GA, 3 months specialized, as little as 2 weeks previews.
   https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations
   Use: the migration treadmill; every model a business built on has a shutdown date.

4. OpenAI pricing page: +10% regional processing (data residency) surcharge on models released on/after 5 Mar 2026.
   https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing
   Use: official price creep via add-on.

5. OpenAI paused new sign-ups and upgrades to the $200/mo ChatGPT Pro plan 10 Sep 2026.
   https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus
   Use: the top tier ran out of supply; subsidy economics straining.

6. OpenAI audited 2025 financials (reported early 2026, FT-verified): revenue $13.07B, total costs $34B, net loss $38.53B (~7.5x the 2024 loss of $5.09B on $3.7B revenue). Cost of serving inference rose $2.65B (2024) to $7.5B (2025).
   https://www.wheresyoured.at/exclusive-openai-financials
   Use: the "heavily subsidised" claim now has audited numbers; the $38.5B hole is the funding source of cheap tokens.

7. Anthropic Opus 4.7, Apr 2026: new tokenizer "may use roughly 1x to 1.35x as many tokens" on the same text at "unchanged" $5/$25 per M = an effective 0-35% price increase on identical prompts, per Anthropic's own migration docs.
   https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/claude-opus-4-7-pricing
   Use: the purest shrinkflation artifact; the unit of account itself shrank.

8. Anthropic Claude Code: the temporary 50% weekly limit boost ends 13 Sep 2026; replaced by a permanent +25% over pre-promo baseline = Anthropic's own words "a 17% reduction in weekly limits" effective 14 Sep 2026.
   https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/claude-code-weekly-limit-reduction-september-14

9. Anthropic Agent SDK / claude -p / GitHub Actions: left subscription usage pools 15 Jun 2026 (announced 13 May 2026), moved to separate monthly credits at API rates; change paused the day it took effect: "Nothing changes for now."
   https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-pauses-claude-agent-sdk-subscription-change
   Use: the flat-rate "unlimited" subsidy being carved out, and the backlash.

10. Anthropic Sonnet 5: launched at "introductory" $2/$10 per M with a scheduled step-up to $3/$15 (50%) on 1 Sep 2026; increase cancelled 10-11 Aug 2026 after backlash.

## B4 — Loss-leader precedents

1. Uber: subsidised both sides for 9 years (driver guarantees "make it almost impossible" to work other platforms, below-taxi fares) — an academic review calls it a "top dog" strategy of pricing below competitors to gain share and limit entry.
   https://journals.openedition.org/rei/6987?lang=en
   The subsidy ended in rate cuts; drivers "contracted loans and bought cars counting on a steady stream of income from the company that later on lowered its payments." By 2021 Uber fares cost 40% more than a year earlier (Rakuten Intelligence, first reported by NYT).
   https://www.competitionpolicyinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/CPI-Abi-Rafeh-Palikot.pdf and https://www.businessinsider.com/uber-lyft-fares-price-driver-shortage-travel-ride-hailing-app-2021-6
   Use: subsidize both sides, buy the market, crank the prices — with a dated, measured increase.

2. Amazon: unprofitable in most years 1998-2013 (first loss since 2014 was 2022, -$2.7B) while funding expansion; AWS is ~18% of revenue but ~40% of total operating profit, peak operating margin 39.5% in Q1 2025.
   https://www.npr.org/2023/02/02/1153562994/amazon-reports-its-first-unprofitable-year-since-2014
   Use: the canonical "lose on the front end, profit on the back end" flywheel.

## B5 — Lock-in / switching costs

1. Satya Nadella, WEF Davos 2026 (with Larry Fink): "Just imagine if your firm, you're not able to embed the tacit knowledge of the firm in a set of weights in a model that you control. By definition, you have no sovereignty. That means you're leaking enterprise value to some model company somewhere."
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1co3zt3-r7I (33:11-33:55)
   Use: the best lock-in quote available; primary source; lands on the "traded long term sovereignty for short term gains" line.

2. Zapier 2026 enterprise survey (via Trantor): 81% of enterprise leaders concerned about AI vendor dependency; only 6% said they could switch providers. Same source: average platform-migration loss $315,000 per project; 57% of IT leaders spending $1M+ on migrations; egress fees 10-15% of a typical enterprise cloud bill (Gartner).
   https://www.trantorinc.com/blog/why-vendor-lock-in
   Use: quantified switching friction; flag the vendor-sourced origin.

3. Anthropic banned third-party tools from drawing on Claude subscription pools (Apr 2026); Claude Code head Boris Cherny: subscriptions "weren't built for the usage patterns of these third-party tools."
   https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-pauses-claude-agent-sdk-subscription-change
   Use: providers actively defining who may consume the subsidised flat rate; the moat around the token.

4. OpenAI deprecation waves force repeated re-migration; community reports allege 4o traffic was "sneakily rerouted to 5.2" ahead of the shutdown.
   https://community.openai.com/t/feedback-on-deprecation-of-chatgpt-4o-feb-17-2026-api-endpoint/1372477
   Use: concrete switching-cost mechanism; every deprecation is a forced re-eval and re-test cycle.

5. Practitioner estimate (label as such): "In 2024, switching providers was a one-line API change. In 2026, it is a 6- to 10-week senior engineering effort: rewriting the agent loop, re-tuning the eval harness, re-validating tool calls, and re-running the compliance review."
   https://teamvoy.com/blog/anthropic-vs-openai
